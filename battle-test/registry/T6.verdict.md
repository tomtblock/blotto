## The per-seat cost is the sharpest number here

The addendum predicted that seating UI-B would push the rent band boundary one seat later, so that
**W07 would be the last seat at 4441080 and W08 the first at 4446160**. Measured, against wallets
funded to exactly 108000000 immediately before the fill:

```
  7 seats sank 104441080  = 100000000 entry + 4441080 pass rent   (W01..W07 = seats 3..9)
 54 seats sank 104446160  = 100000000 entry + 4446160 pass rent   (W08..W61 = seats 10..63)
```

With UI-A, UI-B and W00 at seats 0, 1 and 2 — all three measured at 4441080 — that is **ten seats
in the low band and fifty-four in the high band, with the boundary exactly where it was predicted
to move.** §H20's two bands now hold on a fifth pool, and this is the first time the *boundary
itself* was predicted in advance and then observed to shift.

`T6.reconcile.md` reports this same check as **DRIFT**, and that reading is an artefact: it measures
against the plan's assumed 155000000 starting balance, and the wallet was funded to 108000000. The gap is exactly
`155000000 − 108000000 = 47000000`, and `4441080 + 47000000 = 51441080` — the figure it prints for
every seat. The cost itself is right; the baseline it was compared against is stale.

## Conservation, measured

Treasury **9387645440 at 00:05Z**, before the fill. **15587645440 at 00:27Z**, at harvest.
Difference **6200000000**, which is `62 × 100000000` — exactly the sixty-two entries this run
added, UI-A and UI-B having already paid before the first reading. The 4000000000 prize came from
the vault, which ends at its 650240 rent cushion.

**Both timestamps matter.** Read now the treasury is **11586995200** (00:33Z), which looks like a
contradiction and is not: **pool 28 opened at 00:3xZ and drew 4000650240** from the treasury to
seed its own pot and rent cushion — `4000000000 + 650240` to the lamport. A conservation figure
quoted without its clock is unfalsifiable a few minutes later.

## What the run says about the risk that was taken

W00 scored **877**, the top of its predicted range `[863, 877]`. The top of that range is reached
only when UI-B loses all seven battles to W00 — so the browser tester did **not** concentrate three stats
at 26 or above, and the 34/33/33 case that would have taken rank 1 off W00 did not arise. UI-A and
UI-B tied on **303** at rank 62.

That is luck, not design, and the record should say so. The addendum's shape table showed a
three-stat 34/33/33 scoring 871 and winning outright. Had the browser tester played it, claim 1 would still
have held — it names UI-B as a permitted winner — but the run would have paid 4 SOL to a seat that
was never meant to be in the field.
